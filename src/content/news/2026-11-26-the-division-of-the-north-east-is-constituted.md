On Saturday 7 November 2026 the Knights of Durham and Northumberland walked into the Temple at Gateshead Masonic Hall as two Divisions and walked out as one.

The Most Illustrious Grand Sovereign, M.Ill.Kt. Graham Leslie Flight, G.C.C., came north with his Grand Officers for the occasion. He entered in procession with his Assisting Officers under an arch of steel formed by the Grand Sepulchre Guard, appointed his Officers for the day and told the Knights what they were there to do. He also thanked R.Ill.Kt. Robin Francis Knight, K.C.C., Intendant General of the Durham Division, and gave him his best wishes.

![Grand Officers in front of the new Standard of the Division of the North East](/uploads/north-east-constitution-grand-officers.jpg)

After prayer from the Grand High Prelate, the Grand Sovereign dissolved the Divisions of Durham and Northumberland and constituted the Division of the North East, with authority over nine Conclaves: Royal Kent No. 152, Aycliffe No. 190, Heabrym No. 285, King Oswald No. 316, Bishop Acca No. 333, Amity Stanley No. 338, Earl of Scarbrough No. 383, Astley No. 452 and Prince Bishop Conclave of Improvement No. 492.

Then came the roll call. The Conclave standards were ranged in the West and, as the Grand Recorder read each name, the members of that Conclave stood and bowed to the East while their standard was raised. Every one of our nine Conclaves was well represented, and the Grand Recorder said so.

R.Ill.Kt. Trevor Fish, Intendant General for Northumberland since February 2023, was presented by the Grand Marshal as Intendant General designate. His Patent of Appointment was read and he took his Obligation. The Grand Sovereign handed him the Statutes of the Order and his Patent and placed him on his throne. The Grand Herald proclaimed him and the Knights saluted him to a fanfare from the Grand Organist. Our new Divisional Standard was dedicated straight afterwards.

The Intendant General's first act was to appoint P.Kt. Mark Julian Gilpin as his Deputy. The Division then adopted its by-laws and elected Ill.Kt. Jim Arthur Palfreyman as Treasurer. The Intendant General addressed the Grand Sovereign, who retired in procession with his Assisting Officers.

That left the Division to its own business. The Intendant General appointed his Officers for 2026–2027, among them Ill.Kt. George Parkinson as Eusebius, P.Kt. John David Watts as Senior General, P.Kt. John Forster Baldwin as Junior General, Ill.Kt. David Leslie Armstrong as High Prelate, W.Kt. Paul McKay as Recorder and Ill.Kt. John Johnston Stevenson as Marshal. He then addressed Divisional Conclave for the first time and closed it.

![Knights of the Division of the North East with the Grand Officers after the ceremony](/uploads/north-east-constitution-group.jpg)

Our thanks go to the Grand Sovereign for his attendance and to the Grand Team for a wonderful ceremony. Especial mention must go to the Grand Sepulchre Guard, whose perambulations were splendid throughout, and to the Grand Marshal, Paul Anthony Norman, G.C.C., whose delivery was sublime.

We were delighted to welcome the Provincial Grand Masters of Northumberland and of Durham in the Craft, along with several other Heads of Order. Having both Provincial Grand Masters with us on the day the two Divisions became one meant a great deal.

Thank you to every Knight who attended and to everyone who worked to make the day what it was.
