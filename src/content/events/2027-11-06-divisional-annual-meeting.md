The Annual Meeting of the Division of the North East will be held on **Saturday 6 November 2027** under the Banner of [Royal Kent Conclave No. 152](/conclaves/royal-kent/) at Freemasons' Hall, Alexandra Road, Gateshead, NE8 1RB.

Timings, dress, dining arrangements and the summons will be circulated by the Divisional Recorder nearer the date. All Knights of the Division are warmly invited.
