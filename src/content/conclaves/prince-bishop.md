Prince Bishop Conclave of Improvement No. 492 meets at the Masonic Hall, Chester-le-Street, at 7.00 pm on the fifth Wednesday of the month (January to June and September to December). Enthronements are held between September and December. See the [Meetings Diary](/diary/) for confirmed dates.

Visitors are warmly welcome. For dining returns and visiting enquiries, please contact the Recorder.
